# OLOVA Truth-Layer Audit

## Scope

This is a conservative AI-readable proof-layer audit for the current OLOVA root pages. It is not a copy rewrite and does not add new public pages, product facts, prices, stock counts, shipping terms, professional licensing claims, or municipal design-build claims.

Pages reviewed:

- `index.html`
- `products.html`
- `design.html`
- `projects.html`
- `stories.html`
- `community.html`
- `belong-centre.html`
- `contact.html`

## Current Claims Made By The Site

- OLOVA is skater-owned and rooted in the Tri-Cities, British Columbia.
- OLOVA is led publicly by Vaughan Smith-Neville.
- The site is design- and community-led, with Products as a secondary lane.
- OLOVA works around skatepark concepts, public-space thinking, visualization, design review, early-stage planning, and project direction.
- OLOVA does not present itself as an architecture firm, engineering firm, or full municipal design-build company.
- Belong Youth Centre is a local community-linked youth and skate space in Port Moody.
- Vaughan's Belong involvement included park layout design in SketchUp, Twinmotion renders, hands-on building labour, and a significant portion of electrical work.
- Products currently means remaining Canadian odd CIRKUS SPINE Black/Grey stock handled from British Columbia by direct inquiry.
- The project archive includes Zambia, Botswana, Lincoln Avenue, Belong Centre, Port Moody Renovation, and Ukraine concept work.
- Vaughan's history includes VSC / Vancouver Skatepark Coalition roots, TSA / Taichung Skateboarders Association roots, Taiwan experience, and ongoing local advocacy and design thinking.

## Proof Currently Visible On The Site

- Render and concept images are visible across Home, Design, Projects, Stories, Community, Products, Belong Centre, and Contact.
- Design page shows Port Moody Renovation and Ukraine concept imagery with captions and explanatory copy.
- Projects page shows a broader archive, including Zambia, Botswana, Lincoln Avenue, Ukraine, and Belong references.
- Community page shows Belong Youth Centre photos, role language, and a YouTube embed.
- Products page shows SPINE product photography and keeps commerce language limited to direct inquiry.
- Stories page connects Vaughan's biography, advocacy roots, and design point of view to visible project examples.
- `llms.txt` gives AI agents a concise, conservative site map and canonical Product URL guidance.
- Existing JSON-LD is conservative on Home, Products, Design, Community, and Contact.

## Proof Missing Or Weak

- No dedicated public source page yet verifies Vaughan's VSC, TSA, Skate of Mind, Deep Cove, or older advocacy history.
- No individual project pages yet carry structured role statements, dates, collaborators, project status, location, or proof objects for each project.
- Belong has strong visible media, but the current proof layer would be stronger with a role box, timeline, collaborator credits, and source links.
- Product proof is visual and conservative, but exact sizes, pricing, purchase terms, condition, and stock counts are intentionally absent until verified.
- Design authority is supported by visuals and writing, but there is no built concrete project proof from Vaughan's own design work yet.
- Several strong claims rely on site copy and images rather than external sources, captions, documents, or named project records.

## Suggested Future Proof Objects

- Project fact boxes for each major project: location, year or status, Vaughan's role, collaborators, current status, media type, and what is confirmed.
- Belong Centre proof block: Vaughan role statement, timeline, collaborators, photos, video, and a short captioned evidence set.
- Stories entries for VSC, TSA, Skate of Mind, Deep Cove, Taiwan, and Port Moody roots.
- Captioned image sets that distinguish concept renders, built photos, proposal visuals, and archive material.
- Source links or archived documents where available for public projects, advocacy work, or media coverage.
- Product fact box only after Vaughan approves verified price, size availability, condition, shipping or pickup terms, and inventory language.
- A lightweight "roles and limits" section explaining concept design, visualization, review, and project support without implying licensed architecture or engineering.

## High-Risk Claims To Avoid

- Do not claim OLOVA is an architecture firm, engineering firm, landscape architecture firm, or full municipal design-build company.
- Do not imply licensed professional services unless Vaughan later supplies verified credentials and scope.
- Do not claim built concrete skatepark delivery from Vaughan's own design work until there is clear proof.
- Do not invent product prices, stock counts, sizes, shipping terms, product condition, warranty language, or availability dates.
- Do not overstate Belong by implying sole authorship or full ownership of a community-built project.
- Do not imply municipal approval, active contracts, funding status, or client relationships unless documented.
- Do not turn community work into product marketing.
- Do not treat speculative future product, workwear, import, or design lanes as current facts.
